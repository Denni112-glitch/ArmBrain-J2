About Me

I'm Denis Long — a builder from South Africa obsessed with making robots that actually work outside a lab.

I design and build robotic arms from scratch: CAD in Fusion 360, printing in PETG-CF, wiring, coding and testing until it moves. No team, no lab, just my workshop, a 3D printer, and a lot of failed prints.

ArmBrain J2 is my shoulder joint — the hardest joint in a 6-DOF arm because it carries all the weight. I went with a cycloidal gearbox for zero backlash and high torque, because harmonic drives are too expensive for real builders.

I'm looking for internships, collaborations and people who want to build real hardware. If you're into robotics, mechatronics or just like seeing things move — let's talk.
