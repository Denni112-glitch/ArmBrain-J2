About Me

I'm Denis Long — a builder from South Africa obsessed with making robots that actually work.

I design and build robotic arms from scratch: CAD in Fusion 360, printing in PETG-CF, wiring, coding and testing until it moves. No team, no lab, just my workshop and a lot of failed prints.

ArmBrain J2 is the hardest joint — the shoulder that carries all the weight. I went with a cycloidal gearbox for zero backlash and high torque, because harmonic drives are too expensive for real builders.
